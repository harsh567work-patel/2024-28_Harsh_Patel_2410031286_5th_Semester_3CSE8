> An 8-week industry-oriented virtual internship focused on the complete Data Science and Machine Learning lifecycle, from problem identification and data engineering to model deployment, monitoring, and enterprise AI platform administration.

---

## 📌 Overview

This repository documents my learning, practical exposure, and outcomes from the **Data Science Master Virtual Internship**, conducted under the **AICTE – EduSkills Virtual Internship Program** and supported by **Siemens**.

The internship was designed to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge of Data Science and real-world industry practices. The program provided hands-on exposure to data engineering, machine learning, advanced data preparation, model deployment, monitoring, and enterprise AI platform administration.

Throughout the internship, I worked with industry-relevant workflows using **Python**, **RapidMiner Studio**, and **RapidMiner AI Hub**.

---

## 🎯 Internship Objectives

The key objectives of this internship were:

* Identify business problems suitable for Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning solutions.
* Understand professional data science project scoping and lifecycle management.
* Perform data access, transformation, and preparation.
* Build Machine Learning models for classification and regression tasks.
* Understand model evaluation and performance metrics.
* Apply advanced data preparation and feature engineering techniques.
* Explore ensemble learning and hyperparameter optimization.
* Understand cross-validation and model selection strategies.
* Learn model deployment and monitoring workflows.
* Gain exposure to enterprise AI platform administration.
* Understand concepts such as data drift, retraining, governance, and workflow scheduling.
